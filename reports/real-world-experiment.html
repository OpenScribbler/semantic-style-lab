Corpus design

Sixty hand-labeled contexts from pinned upstream revisions expand into 140 fixtures: 70 development and 70 held out. Eighty fixtures deliberately use a counterfactual spelling. Every surface variant of one sentence stays in one split.

Why v2 improved

Code now handles obvious grammar before inference: words such as by, and, are, and itself cannot be the noun modified by the target. Jev then answers a narrower question that distinguishes set up repositories—verb plus direct object—from setup instructions—modifier plus noun.

Regression checks

The original synthetic suite passed all 90 repeated policy decisions, and the end-to-end adversarial sample still surfaced all five seeded violations.

Held-out failure cases

  • setup-02: setup in “The behavior does vary depending on exactly how your cluster is setup.”
  • setup-02: set-up in “The behavior does vary depending on exactly how your cluster is set-up.”
  • setup-16: set-up in “Organizations can set-up invoicing to sponsor accounts.”

These failures remain routed for review. The report does not recommend automatic editing.

Limitations

One reviewer labeled a deliberately selected corpus; there is no inter-annotator agreement score and this is not a random sample. Excerpts are lightly normalized, and the experiment covers only three term families.
