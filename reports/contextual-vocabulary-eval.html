What to test next

Freeze these prompts, then add real prose without tuning against it: at least 100 labeled occurrences from multiple documentation projects. Include headings, links, tables, quotations, UI strings, product names, punctuation boundaries, and words that can be either nouns or verbs. Measure violation precision and recall separately, calibrate thresholds on a development split, and keep a held-out set.
